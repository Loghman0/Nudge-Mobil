# Nudge Play Store listing and test brief

**App name** Nudge

**Short description** Reflect on what matters and make everyday choices with more clarity.

**Full description** Take a moment to answer a question about how you think, what you enjoy, or what matters to you. Revisit your answers and explore patterns over time.

When you face a choice, use Decide to compare options with your own priorities and ratings. Keep a journal of decisions, reflect on how they turned out, and save priorities you want to use again. You can also turn on a daily question notification on Android.

**Listing inputs** Use the Nudge app icon from `mobile/assets/icon.png`. Capture screenshots of Today, About you, and Decide from the signed build with a disposable account. The current privacy URL is `https://projectnudge.vercel.app/privacy`. Confirm delivery to `projectnudge-support@googlegroups.com` before listing it. Provide a working public account-deletion request URL and verify it with a disposable account before submitting the Data safety form.

**Closed test tasks** Install through Play, complete onboarding, sign in with Google, save a daily answer, save and reuse a decision priority, and remove the saved priority. Repeat on Wi-Fi and mobile data. Open the noon notification and confirm it opens the displayed question. Check the comparison flow with TalkBack and large text. Report the device, Android version, build number, and steps for any failure. Use sample answers when capturing evidence.

**Submission status** The direct-install APK uses a preview signature. The Play submission still needs an upload-signed AAB, a Play Console app and test track, verified contact and privacy details, and installed-device evidence. Confirm the tester requirement on the actual Play account.
