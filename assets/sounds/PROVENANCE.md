# Interaction sounds

Source: [Kenney UI Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio), version 1.0 (2012), downloaded 2026-11-10 from the creator's official download endpoint. License: CC0; original license included here.

Bundled originals: `Audio/mouseclick1.ogg` and `Audio/mouserelease1.ogg`. No audio generation or pitch effect. Playback volume is limited to 0.20; sound preference and OS silent mode are respected. No microphone or background playback permission is needed.

These two mechanically named samples were selected instead of synth/confirmation/glitch sounds. A listening review on the owner's phone remains part of acceptance; waveform/asset checks alone do not prove subjective sound quality.
